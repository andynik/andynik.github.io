### STEM Projects

In 2016 I co-founded [_Kvanta_](https://kvanta.xyz), a year-round STEM programme with **1,000+ learners**, **70+ instructors**, and **$250K+** in funding from Emergent Ventures, XTX Markets, GlobalLogic, and Admixer. We run Olympiad-level courses and boot camps (up to 300+ participants) in mathematics, programming, and science.
